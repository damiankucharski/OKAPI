## Diagnosing changing experts and unreliable references

- On two unseen combinations of expert reliability, giving a shared-weight network 32 unlabeled observations that included a stable third sensor reduced its Brier from 0.1262 to 0.0966 across eight paired seeds. Selecting the expert most often agreeing with that sensor scored 0.0941. [context student](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/context-student/README.md) · [context stress](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/context-stress/README.md) · [context crossings](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/context-crossings/README.md)
- Recovering class probabilities from unlabeled measurements. Across 12 weakly separated Gaussian mixture banks, selecting an EM start and iteration on 1,024 held-out unlabeled rows reduced excess Brier from 0.07120 to 0.01731. Moment initialization alone scored 0.01447. [joint moments](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/joint-moments/README.md) · [translated priors](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/translated-priors/README.md) · [covariance transport](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/covariance-transport/README.md) · [weak mixtures](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/weak-mixtures/README.md)
- Small target-label budgets for auditing changes or selecting a predictor. [sentinel audit](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/sentinel-audit/README.md) · [stratified audit](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/stratified-audit/README.md) · [mixture sentinels](https://github.com/damiankucharski/fusionbench/blob/5f9254e0c6e4559d7c0d19f28dc91641fba09d57/docs/ensemble-study/mixture-sentinels/README.md)
