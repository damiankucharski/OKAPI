# Synchronous migration between OKAPI populations

Freeze this design before experimental scores. A migratory elite can spread a useful subprogram across populations or replace independent alternatives too early.

## Arms and accounting

Compare four isolated populations of six, a four-island directional ring, and one panmictic population of 24. Start all three from the same 24 individuals (three singleton and three seeded shallow compositions per island). Within a paired seed the isolated and migration arms use the same four search RNG starts. On a migration round simultaneously copy one fit elite from each island into its next neighbor. Reject inadmissible or duplicate immigrants, score each attempted new admissible program on the receiver and apply standard receiver survival. Charge every scored immigrant. Use ordinary OKAPI generations with survivor-fitness reuse, maximum 15 nodes, offspring multiplier two, tournament size two and positive-class Brier. Reserve 120 fit evaluations per island, or 480 for the single population. In a partial terminal generation assess only the affordable prefix of novel candidates and use normal survival on assessed candidates and already-scored parents. Stop migration when any island has exhausted its own allowance.

At each completed round retain an untimed diagnostic snapshot. Secondary time comparison uses the last completed snapshot of each policy available by the fastest arm's terminal search time in its paired bank. Include initialization, variation, selection, final truncated work and migration in the measured search cost. Charge the same four structurally distinct validated candidates per policy and endpoint. Report utilized time and fit calls, latency including validation, and snapshot granularity. Hardware timing is descriptive. Count distinct fit prediction vectors on 32 fit-only rows at the final population, independently of model selection.

## Tasks and decision

Use pinned FusionBench 4cf4810 and its existing TwoPathways and CorrelatedRedundancy shifts. Each bank has 384 fit rows, 256 independent source validation rows, 4096 IID and 4096 shifted test rows. Prior to confirmation choose a single migration period from 2 and 5 rounds using only the mean selected validation Brier over four fresh development data/optimizer seeds (21100–21103), both scenarios equally weighted. Resolve ties toward 5. Confirm on 12 new seed banks (21200–21211), each with a distinct deterministic four-seed optimizer tuple. Hold the simulation examples fixed across arms in each bank and use distinct split RNG streams. Interleave arm order across seed banks. All searches and all validation choices finish before either test distribution is drawn. A simple arithmetic mean and full-budget logistic stacker provide context.

Primary contrast is migration minus isolated-islands IID Brier on TwoPathways at exactly 480 fit evaluations plus four validation evaluations. Report two-sided paired 95% t intervals over independent data/optimizer banks, uncorrected secondary shifts/contrasts, accuracy, actual migration attempts/assessments/admissions and decision diversity. An improvement must beat isolation and be competitive with panmixia. Report any failure to meet that criterion.
