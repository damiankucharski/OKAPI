# Retrospective Top-N audit of two published guided-search studies

Use the released public FusionBench `averaging.TopN(metric="brier")` and its documented prefix/tie conventions. Add this baseline to the previously evaluated original banks 25100–25111 (TwoPathways and CorrelatedRedundancy) and transfer banks 28100–28111 (WeakestLink and Parity). Each bank's original four splits, anonymization and GP scores are fixed. The existing outcomes were available when writing this plan. No search method or data split is retuned.

Call the original `experiments.guided.run.data` function for every split. Fit Top-N on the same 384 fit labels as the stacker and GP. Record the public ordering, prefix scores, selected N, provider count, elapsed fit and inference, individual and prefix scans. Fit the original C=1 supervised logit stacker and recompute the all-provider average on these exact rows. Require every bank's archived mean and stacker Brier to match to absolute 1e-12 and require identical accuracy on fit, validation, IID and shift. Freeze the selection before drawing validation or test. Validation provides diagnostics and selects no hyperparameters.

Compare Top-N, all-provider mean, logistic stacking, native GP, shuffled templates and guided GP. Read the original 160/640-call GP assessments verbatim. Average the two GP starts per bank before computing method-minus-Top-N paired differences and unadjusted Student 95% intervals over the 12 independent banks within each family. Report fit/validation/IID/shift Brier, IID/shift accuracy, selected-N distribution, measured latency, actual labels and scan counts, and baselines that outperform the proposed search. Use one thread and record the exact FusionBench commit used. The added baseline is retrospective; no prospective superiority claim follows.
