# Can anonymous provider outputs guide the initial GP population?

## Hypothesis and controls

The stock OKAPI grammar contains an achievable TwoPathways tree whose IID Brier was .06744 with the generator's true groups. This study withholds all grouping information. It tests whether training-set correlations propose useful subtrees and whether those subtrees improve the quality reached within a fixed search budget.

Use two independent FusionBench families, TwoPathways and CorrelatedRedundancy, pinned at `4cf4810e9876f51837b6eba1c4d7aa4bf1c4dac7`. For each of 12 fresh data banks (25100–25111), fit on 384 examples, measure on 256 independent validation examples, and freeze winners before opening 4,096 fresh IID and 4,096 shifted test examples. Use the established weaker-detector or lower-correlation shift. Permute provider columns once per bank and replace semantic names by anonymous IDs. The same permutation binds all splits. Cross each bank with two optimizer starts (311 and 751); average the starts before computing paired t intervals across banks.

## Proposal

On the fit prediction matrix only, cluster columns using average-linkage agglomeration with distance 1 minus their Pearson correlation; treat a constant column as having zero off-diagonal correlation. Consider cuts with one, two, three or four groups. Every group becomes an arithmetic mean over its members. For each cut, enumerate every commutatively distinct binary tree over the groups, using either min or max at each internal node. This preselected grammar deliberately favors small compositions of means, minima and maxima. It can be misspecified. It uses no latent variables, generator groups, labels for clustering, validation information, learned thresholds or test data. The 135 templates (1+2+12+120), including functionally duplicate programs, each incur a full fit assessment. The **placebo** applies one random permutation of provider assignments across the same hierarchy before building its templates. It shares cut sizes, node types, shapes, template count, evaluation cost and label access.

## Budgets and endpoints

Compare native singleton-initialized GP, the correlation-guided initialization and the randomized-template initialization. All use the actual stock six operators, K initial singletons and a population of K, offspring multiplier 2, tournament size 2, no node/depth cap and ordinary survivor rescoring. After scoring the K singletons, each template arm scores its 135 templates and applies ordinary OKAPI survival to the combined pool. All arms retain the best full-precision fit Brier observed, with ties favoring first observation. Deploy that same incumbent at exactly 160 and 640 total fit calls, including initializations and partial generation prefixes. Validation measures the frozen incumbent and never chooses it. Separately report mean and regularized logit stacking (C=1) fitted on the same fit rows, with zero validation selection. Include time to build, score and select proposals, search time, evaluation counts and descriptive validation/inference timing.

The primary contrast is guided minus placebo fresh IID Brier at 160 assessments on TwoPathways. Report all four-split Briers, paired intervals, win counts, between-start and between-bank variability, and the corresponding late-budget and shifted contrasts. Compare directly with native GP, mean and stacking. Report the control task even if the structured proposal hurts there. Early and late scores measure convergence over these two fixed budgets, without a claim of a global optimum. The task-specific achievable tree remains a published reference point, not an input to the initializer.
