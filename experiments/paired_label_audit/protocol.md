# Finite-pool comparison of two frozen binary forecasts

This is an algebra and implementation audit for the prospective FusionBench validation study. Fix the two probability vectors f and g on N known rows before requesting any of their binary labels. The estimand is their difference in positive-class empirical Brier, f minus g, on this finite pool. We will audit two separate decisions made with expensive labels.

1. Compare uncertainty under independent record sampling with replacement, using a known offset and an unbiased estimate of the remaining contrast. Compare uniform draws with probability proportional to the absolute forecast difference. Derive its fixed-draw variance, exact sign-count distribution, treatment of ties and repeats, and an example delimiting the optimality claim.
2. With adversarially unspecified binary labels on the remaining rows, give a prediction interval containing every possible completion of the full-pool difference. Check if deterministic querying by absolute difference minimizes the worst-case interval width for a fixed budget of distinct rows.

Before calculation, fix the exact enumeration to three rows whose probabilities take values in {0, 1/2, 1}, all ordered forecast pairs and all eight label vectors. Compare the risk identity, variances, endpoint attainability, worst-case deterministic width, and exact binomial law using rational arithmetic; a separate non-grid counterexample may be used. This checker uses no study datasets, fitted trees or outcome-informed selection. FusionBench owns the independent training banks, three predictive baselines and any real label-budget comparison. The existing observer estimates context prevalence from disagreement on unlabelled inputs and uses source validation losses for selection.
