# Descriptive exposure check

The first pass of the registered availability audit found no increase in strict productive joins after adding internal accumulators. Before calculating the exposure quantities below, we added a descriptive analysis to distinguish lack of simultaneous use from complete absence of the relevant pieces. It reuses the same traces and snapshots. It is supplementary to the registered endpoint.

For each G1/G5/G10/G15 matched live and historical state count whether zero, exactly one, or both of the generating conjunctions `MIN(A,B)` and `MIN(C,D)` are present among target-free sources under each of the four registered extraction policies. With both present, distinguish whether any two different source genotypes own them. Other ways to join two donors into the target remain included in the primary audit. On both laws count states that actually contain a ValueNode with two or more sequential operations in a target-free program, and whether a proper prefix adds a new semantic donor to the available support when compared to completed and raw nodes. Distinguish novel functions from extra ways to obtain an already available function from another genotype. These measures inspect existing traces without fitting or changing a population.
