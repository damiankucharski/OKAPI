# Matched training loss and generalization of ordinary OKAPI

Freeze this protocol before measuring outcomes. Use fresh independent data banks 24100–24111 on the pinned FusionBench 4cf4810 TwoPathways (eight providers) and CorrelatedRedundancy (ten providers) generators. Compare nested fit sizes 96, 384 and 1,536, using prefixes of a single freshly generated fit split in each bank, 256 independent validation examples, 4,096 IID examples and 4,096 shifted examples. Keep validation, IID and shift examples shared across fit sizes and methods. Shift detector accuracy .90→.78 or block correlation .85→.10; use fixed model and weights. Under each condition all methods receive identical columns and rows. Test inputs and oracle probabilities enter analysis only after model selection.

**Primary comparisons.** Arithmetic mean uses all providers and zero fit labels. FusionBench StackingLogit standardizes logit features on the fit rows and fits its ordinary L2 logistic regression with C=1, without hyperparameter search or validation refit. Ordinary OKAPI starts from its native singleton population (all providers), default six operators, ordinary crossover/mutation, node-count Pareto selection, no operator additions, caps, migration, local refinement, cached-survivor shortcut or custom composition initialization. Use Brier loss, population multiplier two and tournament size two. Trace two predetermined independent GP seeds per bank. From the first seed use the fit-selected frontier champion after 8 and 48 generations, without validation selection. At 48 generations also form a two-seed policy that chooses between the two fit champions using only validation Brier. Use the same seeds across fit sizes in each bank. Record score/complexity/calls/time at both checkpoints, plus validation cost of the two-seed policy. Fresh fit and validation diagnostics of the already fixed models do not change their choices.

**Falsifiable diagnostic.** At N=384 the primary paired contrast is 48-generation fixed-seed GP minus same-fit stacking in Brier on fit and fresh IID, separately per family. Report both means and 95% t intervals over 12 independent data/optimizer banks. Also report every split, all fit sizes, shift, paired change in the stack-minus-GP ordering with more generations, and the excess generalization gap (GP IID-minus-fit gap less the stack's). The four prespecified primary contrasts are reported separately without multiplicity adjustment. The study can distinguish an existing empirical fit gap from a gap that appears only on held-out data. An empirical fit gap alone does not identify an optimizer failure or a fundamental limit of all trees.

**Mechanism controls.** The equal mean is feasible with one mean node. Also supply a fixed feasible source-informed tree on each family: the maximum of two paths, each the minimum of two pairwise detector means, or a weighted arithmetic pool with source covariance-informed positive weights proportional to 1/(1+7*.85) on each redundant provider and 1 on each independent provider. These trees know provider groups and are diagnostics outside the deployable fair contest. A better feasible tree exposes search headroom at these budgets. The existing six stock operators, for binary probabilities and identity postprocessing, preserve the closed interval between the smallest and largest provider probabilities on every row. For held-out domains clip the known generator posterior q to the per-row interval. Report the floor E[(q−clip(q))²] and GP's measured E[(p−q)²]. This floor bounds any interval-valued predictor's excess Brier; the residual can combine search error and additional representation constraints. Never pass q or the source grouping control to any fitted competitor.
