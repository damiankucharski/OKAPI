# Can initialization already put the solution one native swap away?

This retrospective analysis uses the completed eight-provider geometry experiment with 12 data banks 7200–7211, two search starts 907/919, three initializers (STOCK, CHAIN, BRANCH) and two MIN/MAX conditional laws (BRANCH, CHAIN). The original results were known before this registration, including the BRANCH-law target appearing by 1,200 calls in 21/24 BRANCH, 11/24 CHAIN and 2/24 STOCK searches. Only one of the 21 BRANCH hits was already scored at initialization. No new G0 crossover probabilities have been measured. The additive mean has no exact MIN/MAX target and is outside the mechanistic endpoint. [Source registration and existing controls](https://github.com/damiankucharski/FusionBench/blob/32ca87e/docs/ensemble-study/seed-geometry/protocol.md)

For each of 144 starting populations retain all eight singletons and 30 scored seeds in their original order, including structurally or functionally equal individuals. Use the original fit predictions and labels to reproduce the native rounded 1−Brier scalar scores. Compare all initial genotypes, their unrounded semantics and fit-winner losses to the archived records. Calculate the exact conditional law of the next crossover before any mutation or selection of a new generation. Native tournaments take five distinct candidates in random order, select the closest to the fixed ideal point with the first winning ties, and choose the two parents independently. The uncapped native exchange selects the kind of node uniformly among kinds available in both parents, then one site of that kind in each. Count either offspring, counted once when both compute target, and include self mating. G0 allows one newly formed target only if both parents were target-free. Compare to uniform parent choice keeping all sites and offspring fixed.

The primary count is new legal one-swap opportunities in the G0 BRANCH-initialized populations on the BRANCH law whose initial candidates did not contain the true rule and whose 1,200-call archive eventually did. Report the exact numerator/denominator and distinguish a zero legal probability, a positive legal probability suppressed to zero by the real tournament, and a positive native probability. Include all three initializers, the CHAIN-law control, both found/later-missed groups, and the original G0 target-present cases. Summarize the native and uniform per-draw probabilities on all G0 target-absent cells, including zeros. Bank-level paired intervals for BRANCH minus CHAIN use both GP starts per bank and all cells, including any already containing the target, always excluding target-holding parent pairs. The postselected eventual-hit subset describes archived paths and has no causal interpretation.

Check child pairs against the actual native RNG support in both offspring on G0 slots (0,8), (8,9), (17,33), (37,37) per initializer and search seed, using all 256 corners. Verify turn weights by direct enumeration on smaller tournament fixtures. These probabilities characterize the unmutated next proposal from the specified G0. Determining the actual ancestral path of a later scored hit would require a population lineage ledger. The source experiment owns the frozen three predictive baselines, fit/validation/IID/shift and search costs. This audit makes no new training or held-out selection decisions.
