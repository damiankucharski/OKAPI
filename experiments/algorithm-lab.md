# OKAPI algorithm lab

## Initial priorities and decision criteria

| Priority | Mechanism and current evidence | Cost | Risk and decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reuse fitness for surviving, unchanged parents. The existing loop scores every retained parent again after each crossover and mutation round. | One opt-in flag and selection-mask bookkeeping. | Require deterministic fitness and frozen inputs. Retain only if candidate predictions and population trajectories agree exactly and objective calls fall. |
| 2 | Improve a fitted tree by replacing one provider at a single value-node occurrence, preserving its operators and weights. Current mutations change parameters, add a provider or delete a branch. Inference substitution currently replaces *all* occurrences together. | Up to 32 additional fit-loss evaluations including the incumbent and two validation evaluations. | A niche selected on fit can disappear in a new environment. Compare with ordinary GP continuing under exactly the same allowance. |
| 3 | Trim one low and one high score before averaging, with normalization for multiclass probabilities. Available min/max/threshold and logit operators serve other purposes. | Additional reductions over providers, two extra temporary tensors in a streaming implementation. | A unique correct specialist can be discarded. Require a fault-free specialist control and correlated-failure trials alongside intermittent single-provider faults. |
| 4 | On pre-training data, group duplicate predictions and describe disagreement and missing/invalid data. For future fitness or sampling, validate channel reliability on independent labels. | Pairwise comparisons scale quadratically in provider count. | Identical fit predictions can diverge later. The existing context diagnostics, sentinel statistics and intervention tools already address parts of the problem. Use disagreement as a diagnostic until an action beats controls on held-out operating conditions. |
| 5 | Run several smaller populations, exchange distinct programs periodically and charge fitness re-evaluation on the receiving island. Existing work has tested independent restarts and intermediate archives. | Several populations and migration scheduling. | A good migrant can eliminate diversity. Compare with a single trajectory and isolated islands under matched search and validation budgets. |

## Protocol for provider reassignment

Freeze this design before scoring test data. Use the existing FusionBench TwoPathways and CorrelatedRedundancy scenarios and their search-stability shifts. Use 12 independent data seeds 20200–20211, two GP starts per seed (451 and 452), 256 fit, 256 validation and 4096 examples per test domain. Both methods share an ordinary 8-generation checkpoint evolved using positive-class Brier, six seeded shallow compositions and up to eight singletons, population multiplier 2 and tournament size 3. Cap programs at depth 7 and 21 nodes. On the checkpoint's champion, sample single-occurrence substitutions without replacement, up to 31 proposals plus its full-precision incumbent score. Freeze the best strictly improving move. Ordinary GP gets the identical number of additional fit evaluations on the same input, including rescoring its incumbent. It may nominate its best evaluated program even when an allowance ends mid-generation. For both policies, validate the incumbent and nominee on identical examples, break ties in favor of the incumbent, then open IID and shifted tests. Report positive-class Brier, accuracy, objective-call and validation counts, latency and paired t intervals over data seeds after averaging the two GP starts. Arithmetic mean and a logistic stacker fitted to all 512 available labels provide additional baselines. The primary contrast is refinement minus continued-GP Brier on IID TwoPathways. Look for replication across domains and retain negative controls and reversals.

A second continued-GP control enables the new survivor-fitness option at the same checkpoint. This lets it spend the same additional fit budget on more distinct offspring. Report it beside the primary ordinary-GP control. See the [survivor reuse evaluation](survivor_reuse.md) for the engineering comparison.
