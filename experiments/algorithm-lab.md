# OKAPI algorithm lab

## Required references for every new OKAPI experiment

Run three controls on the same available expert outputs and labeled development rows: the unweighted arithmetic mean, ranked-prefix Top-N averaging, and regularized logistic stacking. For Top-N, rank the single providers by the experiment's primary metric in a deterministic order, score every equally weighted prefix from 1 through M on development data, and freeze the winning prefix and N before opening IID or shifted test labels. Use FusionBench’s shared `TopN` implementation and specify the metric, ranking/selection split, tie policy, chosen N and scoring cost. State the input transform and regularization of the stacker and the exact labels available to every policy. In fit-only GP comparisons all learned baselines get the identical fit rows; report any additional selection labels separately. Historical additions are labeled retrospective. [Retrospective Guided comparison](guided_topn/README.md)

## Initial priorities and decision criteria

| Priority | Mechanism and current evidence | Cost | Risk and decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reuse fitness for surviving, unchanged parents. The existing loop scores every retained parent again after each crossover and mutation round. | One opt-in flag and selection-mask bookkeeping. | Require deterministic fitness and frozen inputs. Retain only if candidate predictions and population trajectories agree exactly and objective calls fall. |
| 2 | Improve a fitted tree by replacing one provider at a single value-node occurrence, preserving its operators and weights. Current mutations change parameters, add a provider or delete a branch. Inference substitution currently replaces *all* occurrences together. | Up to 32 additional fit-loss evaluations including the incumbent and two validation evaluations. | A niche selected on fit can disappear in a new environment. Compare with ordinary GP continuing under exactly the same allowance. |
| 3 | Trim one low and one high score before averaging, with normalization for multiclass probabilities. Available min/max/threshold and logit operators serve other purposes. | Additional reductions over providers, two extra temporary tensors in a streaming implementation. | A unique correct specialist can be discarded. Require a fault-free specialist control and correlated-failure trials alongside intermittent single-provider faults. |
| 4 | On pre-training data, group duplicate predictions and describe disagreement and missing/invalid data. For future fitness or sampling, validate channel reliability on independent labels. | Pairwise comparisons scale quadratically in provider count. | Identical fit predictions can diverge later. The existing context diagnostics, sentinel statistics and intervention tools already address parts of the problem. Use disagreement as a diagnostic until an action beats controls on held-out operating conditions. |
| 5 | Run several smaller populations, exchange distinct programs periodically and charge fitness re-evaluation on the receiving island. Existing work has tested independent restarts and intermediate archives. | Several populations and migration scheduling. | A good migrant can eliminate diversity. Compare with a single trajectory and isolated islands under matched search and validation budgets. |

## Protocol for provider reassignment

Freeze this design before scoring test data. Use the existing FusionBench TwoPathways and CorrelatedRedundancy scenarios and their search-stability shifts. Use 12 independent data seeds 20200–20211, two GP starts per seed (451 and 452), 256 fit, 256 validation and 4096 examples per test domain. Both methods share an ordinary 8-generation checkpoint evolved using positive-class Brier, six seeded shallow compositions and up to eight singletons, population multiplier 2 and tournament size 3. Cap programs at depth 7 and 21 nodes. On the checkpoint's champion, sample single-occurrence substitutions without replacement, up to 31 proposals plus its full-precision incumbent score. Freeze the best strictly improving move. Ordinary GP gets the identical number of additional fit evaluations on the same input, including rescoring its incumbent. It may nominate its best evaluated program even when an allowance ends mid-generation. For both policies, validate the incumbent and nominee on identical examples, break ties in favor of the incumbent, then open IID and shifted tests. Report positive-class Brier, accuracy, objective-call and validation counts, latency and paired t intervals over data seeds after averaging the two GP starts. Arithmetic mean and a logistic stacker fitted to all 512 available labels provide additional baselines. The primary contrast is refinement minus continued-GP Brier on IID TwoPathways. Look for replication across domains and retain negative controls and reversals.

A second continued-GP control enables the new survivor-fitness option at the same checkpoint. This lets it spend the same additional fit budget on more distinct offspring. Report it beside the primary ordinary-GP control. See the [survivor reuse evaluation](survivor_reuse.md) for the engineering comparison.

## Decisions after the first measurements

| Mechanism | Evidence | Decision |
|---|---|---|
| Reuse survivor fitness | 576/576 generation fingerprints matched. The stock and reuse runs returned identical held-out scores. Cheap Brier timing was inconclusive. With sklearn average precision and 32,768 fit examples, mean fit-plus-validation time fell from 1.243 to 0.751 s across eight independent pairs. | Enable explicitly for stable, deterministic scoring with a costly objective. More generations can improve Brier at the cost of additional variation and selection time. [Trajectory and generalization](survivor_reuse.md) · [AP timing](survivor_workload.md) |
| Local reassignment | On TwoPathways, the IID difference against ordinary continuation was −.00237 Brier (95% interval −.00492 to +.00018). The difference against cached continuation was −.00023. | Available as a budgeted post-search experiment. Keep the frozen incumbent in independent validation. Gains over the matched challenger remain uncertain. [Results and API](provider_refinement.md) |
| Trim one at each tail | Across 16 banks, trim-one lowered minority-fault Brier from .13104 to .11606. Shared majority faults reversed the ranking (.19521 to .20807). A lone informative specialist among six neutral outputs lost all influence. | Expose the operator through the explicit operator palette. Before using it, measure held-out loss and classification accuracy under the relevant mix of failures. [Fixed-pool study](trimmed_pool.md) |

## Next experiment designs

* **Islands with migration.** Compare four independent populations with and without one-way ring migration, and one population with the same total size. Send one high-fitness candidate per island at fixed intervals. Copy the tree and rebind its provider IDs to the receiving fit data before evaluating it there. Compare exact clones with distinct prediction patterns to measure early collapse. Use the same objective-evaluation cap and the same number of independently validated finalists in each arm. Record migration assessments and elapsed time. Select the migration interval on development seeds before opening confirmation banks.
* **Prediction analysis before search.** On fit data, record per-provider shape, nonfinite score rate, probability normalization, calibration on known labels, duplicates, pairwise disagreement and residual correlation. Cross-fit any learned screening rule and test it against keeping every provider, plus a label-free flag-only baseline. Include an accurate minority specialist and a changing correlated block as counterexamples. Where independent labels are scarce, report uncertainty for a proposed provider exclusion. Distribution overlap can make defective and useful providers observationally indistinguishable on fit data.
* **Value of operator diversification.** Cross the trimmed-mean palette with a default-palette control in an actual GP run under an equal wall-time and objective-call budget. The fixed pooling experiment measures what a specified operator does to the same seven inputs. Evolved trees may repeat provider IDs, alter arity, or concentrate a shared error, so their behavior needs a separate comparison.

## Research journal

* **Ring migration.** Across 12 fresh TwoPathways banks at exactly 480 fit evaluations, four communicating islands achieved .15238 IID Brier, four isolated islands .15908 and one equally large population .14692. Migration improved on isolation, while one population remained preferable here. The optional helper and receiver-side cost accounting are available for experiments that require separated populations. [Protocol, per-bank results and timing control](islands/README.md)
* **How much of the stacking gap is already on fit?** With 384 identical labeled fit rows, GP48 trailed stacking on fit by .02634 Brier in TwoPathways and .03061 in CorrelatedRedundancy. Fresh IID gaps were .02795 and .03241. A 15-node achievable program exposed considerable search headroom on TwoPathways. [Prospective learning curves](fit_gap/README.md)
* **Data-guided initialization.** From anonymous fit outputs, average-link clustering and a bounded library of 135 min/max compositions reached .06715 IID Brier on TwoPathways at 160 full fit assessments. Random membership with the same tree shapes gave .14700, native GP .18521, and matched stacking .13398. The guided-versus-random difference on CorrelatedRedundancy disappeared by 640 calls. [Benchmark and opt-in API](guided/README.md)
* **Shared Gaussian error.** When nuisance pairs were more correlated than true concept pairs, a fourth-order grouping score on logits recovered the concepts in all 12 banks, compared with zero for ordinary Pearson correlation. At 1,536 fit rows the two libraries obtained .15143 and .19770 IID Brier under the same assessment budget. The statistic relies on a non-Gaussian concept and Gaussian nuisance on the logit scale. [Registered counterexample and paired outcomes](guided_stress/README.md)
* **Two observationally indistinguishable groupings.** With identical unlabeled providers in two worlds, a single top matching recovered 12 of 24 labeled tasks. Retaining the top two plausible matchings and scoring their compositions on source labels recovered all 24. IID Brier averaged .15023 (two ranked matchings), .19202 (two random matchings), .21389 (native GP) and .18406 (stacking). The ranked and random policies both used 240 fit and four validation assessments. [Identification limit and counterexample](ambiguous_groups/README.md)
* **Exact library compression.** With identity node postprocessing, min and max commute with thresholding. Truth tables over up to four groups compress the 135-template library to 63 function representatives. The first Brier and AP winners agreed on all 20 new banks. AP construction plus scoring averaged 101 vs 203 ms on TwoPathways and 99 vs 210 ms on CorrelatedRedundancy. Later GP can still respond to changed representation multiplicity. [Proof, budget and timings](guided_equivalence/README.md)
* **Transfer without retuning.** On WeakestLink, early guided IID Brier was .04141 vs .12829 for native GP. On Parity it was .25185 vs .27463, yet fitted class prevalence yielded .25063 and the generator posterior .01136. The current positive min/max library cannot express XOR. [Four-family convergence figure and transfer data](guided_transfer/README.md)
* **Tree identity during search.** The printable key merged different tree programs. In naturally occurring stock GP trajectories, 884 of 918 suppressed distinct candidates changed probe predictions. The corrected key gave a TwoPathways IID Brier difference of −.00051 [−.00686, +.00583] after 640 fit assessments. [Production regression, exact replay and three matched baselines](structural_key/README.md)
* **Nonmonotone Parity.** With two fit-inferred provider groups, an eight-tree library containing probabilistic XOR obtained .01135 IID Brier vs .25085 for a matched substitute. The chosen tree agreed with the known generator posterior in all 12 new Parity banks. No CorrelatedRedundancy bank chose XOR. [Registered mechanism, controls and posterior audit](parity_operator/README.md)
