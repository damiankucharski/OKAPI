## An independent calculation from observable probabilities

For a bank of K providers with C classes, the generator's latent raw channel vector for provider m has mean `s e_y`. Each class has unit variance and the K providers have the equicorrelation matrix Σ with off-diagonal ρ. Observing probabilities retains the C−1 log ratios against the last class. For every provider these coordinates have covariance `I + 11ᵀ`; across providers the full covariance is the Kronecker product `Σ ⊗ (I + 11ᵀ)`. All C possible class means have equal covariance-weighted squared norms. Since `Σ⁻¹1 = 1 / [1+(K−1)ρ]` times the all-ones vector, subtracting the Gaussian log likelihoods for two classes yields `s / [1+(K−1)ρ]` times the sum of their K reported log probability ratios. With uniform class prior the posterior is thus the softmax of this weighted sum of log probabilities. For ρ=1, generated providers coincide and their repeated observations contain exactly one provider's evidence. The limiting expression gives that same posterior.

The verifier evaluates dense multivariate Gaussian densities over the actual observable contrasts, independently of the scenario's oracle. On 192 new generated rows per default, the largest absolute probability difference from the derived formula was 2.6×10⁻¹⁵ for MulticlassCommittee (weight .39605835) and 8.6×10⁻¹⁵ for Saturated (weight .42485941). The old reference differed from the direct density by up to .36542 and .34894 on these two fixtures. Additional controls covered a single provider, intermediate correlation .37, and perfectly duplicated providers. These are discrepancies on the stated draws, with no model selection or claims about their prevalence.

The existing optional `LogitMeanNode` with all K anonymous providers, standard multiclass normalization and `temperature = K s / [1+(K−1)ρ]` reproduced the new default references to 8.6×10⁻¹⁵. This is a known achievable setting that uses the generating parameters. The standard six-operator panel excluded this optional operator. In general its input clipping and output clamps limit exact equality; neither was active on the verified rows. Use the realized correlation after the generator's clipping if a constructor is passed out-of-range values. The `accuracy` parameter sets the generator's logit elevation heuristically in multiclass and is separate from observed top-1 accuracy.
