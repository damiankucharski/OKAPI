# Independent refit on frozen inputs

The transfer and primary nonlinear comparison are already measured. This follow-up tests the additional OKAPI fitting entry point, not another estimate of generalization on new banks. On the same two families, 12 banks, C=1, two transforms and four splits, independently fit the outer node through OKAPI's fit_stacking_tree using only copied fixed roots and 384 training labels. No fitted FusionBench scaler, coefficients or initialization are passed to this route. Freeze native fits before scoring validation or test. Compare every probability to a separately trained public estimator and verify native Brier against the archived native transfer results. Include both explicit physical channel roots and folded positive roots for the original M inputs; also test the native positive-only path on the four-group/three-feature nonlinear library. Reuse the matched arithmetic mean, ranked-prefix Top-N, public p/logit stackers and ordinary GP results from the primary experiment. Report fit timing including feature computation, largest observed error per arm and any violations of 1e-9 on float64. If precision matters, quantify float32 differences separately without extrapolating float64 tolerances to float32. The learned parameters are the outer signed coefficients and intercept; the fixed inner functions receive no gradients.
