# Can grouping separate a shared concept from a shared measurement error?

This independent stress test fixes a four-concept population before opening outcomes. Its target is (A and B) or (C and D). Each concept is an independent balanced sign; two anonymous providers observe each sign through Gaussian measurement noise. Their marginal sign accuracy is .78. A second randomly drawn matching pairs providers from different concepts. The Gaussian measurement errors within each of these pairs have correlation ρ, either 0 or .90. All four latent concept assignments and the nuisance matching stay private to the simulator. Every provider keeps its marginal accuracy, even when the nuisance dominates its pairwise correlation.

**Proposed grouping signal.** Transform probability to log odds, center and standardize each column on fit. For two standardized columns X,Y, estimate the signed score −κ(X,X,Y,Y) = 1 + 2 E[XY]² − E[X²Y²]. For repeated independent Rademacher signals with jointly Gaussian measurement noise, Gaussian fourth cumulants vanish and the population score is positive for two measurements of the same concept and zero for measurements of different concepts. Empirical fourth moments can be noisy. Use the same average-linkage algorithm and the same 135 actual OKAPI templates for each similarity. Compare the fourth-cumulant signal with Pearson correlation of probabilities, Pearson correlation of log odds, random membership with Pearson-derived group sizes, and a diagnostic oracle supplied the true partition solely for a four-group 120-template search. Include equal mean and an L2 logistic logit stacker (C=1) fit on the same rows. All learned groupings are label-free; the winner within each template library uses only labeled fit Brier.

**Budget and endpoints.** Use 12 fresh independent banks 25300–25311, two nested fit sizes (384 and 1,536), and the two source nuisance settings. Separately generate 256 validation and 4,096 IID/4,096 shifted examples. The counterfactual shift switches ρ while holding target definition, provider identity and accuracy fixed. Fit-stage scoring of 135 trees is charged to each learned library; no further GP, refinement or validation selection. Also report grouping costs and fit/inference wall times. New random permutations hide provider IDs in each bank. Score fit/validation/IID/shift, plus the adjusted Rand index of recovered versus true groups at the four-cluster cut and exact group recovery. Unveil group truth only to the evaluator and oracle control.

Primary contrast: fourth-cumulant grouping minus probability-Pearson grouping in IID Brier when fitted at ρ=.90 and N=1,536. Report the paired 95% t interval over independent banks and always report the clean and smaller-fit controls, recovery rates, and frozen shifts. This measures a template initializer alone, distinct from the earlier full-GP budgeted comparison. The simulator deliberately gives the squared-cumulant statistic a known mechanism. Any improvement is conditional on those distributional assumptions.
